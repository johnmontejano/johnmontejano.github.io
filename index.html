→  Before we talk

A few practical
questions.

What exactly am I hiring you to do?

Assess how work moves through your business, simplify repetitive steps and build the agreed automation. That can include customer intake, quote follow-ups, reminders, booking or invoicing. Website design is available when it supports the workflow.

Do I need to replace all my software?

Not necessarily. We start with what you use and check its integration options and access requirements. If a connection is not practical, we discuss the alternatives before agreeing the build.

Is FastFix.ai the service I’m buying here?

FastFix.ai is my own product and an example of what I build. This page is for working with me directly on your business’s workflow; you do not need to sign up for FastFix to start a conversation.

How much does it cost, and how long will it take?

That depends on the workflow, tools and exceptions involved. We agree the scope, cost and timeline before implementation. The initial 30-minute conversation is free.

Will automation make decisions for my team?

Only the repeatable actions we agree should run automatically. Your team keeps control of pricing, approvals and unusual requests. We define permissions and human handoffs before anything goes live.
