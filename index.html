Before you ask
Will this replace my people?

No. It takes the retyping and the chasing off their plate so they can do the work you actually hired them for.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all — you run the business, I build and run the automation. Plain English the whole way, and it lives in accounts you own.

Is this only for construction and trades?

FastFix.ai is my home-services software, but the automation work fits any business where a customer books a time and someone has to show up — clinics, salons, med spas, repair shops, cleaners, property managers.

What does it cost?

The 30-minute call and the map that follows it are free. What a build costs depends entirely on what the map finds, and I'll tell you the number before anything starts.

Have you actually run one of these businesses?

Yes — and I build the software for them. FastFix.ai is mine: booking, quotes and invoices for home-service companies, built end to end. Before that I ran GelatoTech here in San Francisco, where I answered the phone, drove out, did the repair and sent the invoice. Every service business has the same short list of work nobody wants to do — the phone, the chasing, the retyping. That is exactly what I take off you.

What if nothing is worth automating?

Then I'll say so on the call and you keep the map anyway. I would rather lose an hour than build something you don't need.
